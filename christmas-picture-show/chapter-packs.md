### Did you know?

- The film began as Philip Van Doren Stern’s short story The Greatest Gift, which he had printed as a Christmas greeting.

- George's snowy streets were filmed on a studio backlot. The production used an artificial snow mixture that let the actors speak while it fell.

- Frank Capra made the film through Liberty Films. It earned five Academy Award nominations, including Best Picture.

### Did you know?

- The set builders reused scenery from Oliver! to reconstruct Victorian London at Shepperton Studios.

- Ronald Searle illustrated the opening titles. His painted caricatures introduce the world of the film before the actors appear.

- Albert Finney was in his thirties during filming. Makeup allowed him to play both elderly Scrooge and his younger self.
