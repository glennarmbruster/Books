# Books

- [The Last Port Casebook](./last-port-casebook/) — 500 mystery puzzles and a companion answer book.
- [The Prism Cabinet](./prism-cabinet/) — 500 illustrated and text challenges with hints and a companion answer book.
- [Goobie's Survival Guide](./field-and-home/) — the searchable Survival Book, with 769 guides. [Download the Apple Books edition](./field-and-home/Field-and-Home.epub?raw=true).
- [The Desk Dictionary & Thesaurus](./desk-dictionary/) — offline English definitions, sense-grouped synonyms and antonyms, fuzzy spelling search, and regional variants. [Data credits](./desk-dictionary/credits.html).
- [Score Book](./score-book/) — offline round scores, running totals and dated scorecards.

- [Wordplay Café](./wordplay-cafe/) — 300 playable word puzzles (200 word ladders, 50 cryptograms and 50 word hunts) with saved progress.
- [The Card Table](./card-table/) — 100 offline card-game rules, a guided bridge deal and a random game picker.

- [The Maker’s Bench](./makers-bench/) — 295 searchable projects including sports, holidays, seasons, Jeep trails, and ornaments. The offline studio has 7,320 artwork choices, 90 fonts, and a workshop for saving layered designs.

- [Sudoku](./sudoku/) — 100 graded puzzles with pencil notes and saved progress.
- [German Phrase Book](./german-phrase-book/) — 640 searchable travel phrases, pronunciation audio and saved favorites.

- [WEIRD Facts & Trivia](./weird-facts-trivia/) — 20,100 sourced questions, growing in reviewed batches toward a 100,000-question ceiling. New games default to 70% easy and 10% each medium, hard, and obscure; the bank keeps independent difficulty estimates. Choose category, initial, length and percentages, or use Any mix when a narrow selection cannot supply a requested mix. Browse numbered questions in batches of 100 and share an offline round code.

- [Memory Activity Book](./memory-activity-book/) — 250 numbered activities including picture matching, sequence and spatial memory, story recall, word pairs and category recall.

The back-of-book Food Safety Quick Reference appendix includes refrigeration decisions, storage times, freeze-dried product examples, and cat food and treats.
