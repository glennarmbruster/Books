Question sources

Reveal a card to see its source. Questions, answer choices and explanations are included in the offline book. Source websites open when you have a connection.

Difficulty ratings are estimates for a general adult player. On questions with choices or a clue, the rating assumes you can see them.

Reviewed additions

Questions 20,001–30,000 are additions published in reviewed batches checked against the references linked on each card. The latest release adds 7,400 questions, numbered 22,601–30,000, with per-card sources. Difficulty labels are estimates for adult players, not measured test scores. Letter rounds hide the choices and can therefore feel harder.

The collection will grow in reviewed batches toward a maximum of 100,000 questions. New games default to 70% easy, with the remainder split equally among medium, hard and obscure. The collection itself has no fixed difficulty quota.

Museums and heritage

Selected questions use the Cleveland Museum of Art’s CC0 open-access collection data. Heritage questions adapt UNESCO’s World Heritage List and Intangible Heritage List datasets. These UNESCO adaptations use original question wording and are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0, with attribution to UNESCO and a link to the relevant entry on each card. Science Museum Group collection-record adaptations credit the museum on each card and use its CC BY 4.0 open collection data; question wording has been adapted. Other museum, scientific and educational references are credited on their individual cards. No endorsement by these institutions is implied.

Trivia collections

Cards from Open Trivia Database, OpenQuizzDB, and OpenTriviaQA credit their contributors on the cards. Their questions and available anecdotes have been selected, edited for presentation, and paired with shuffled answer choices. These adaptations are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Curiosity archives

Archive cards adapt facts and article introductions by Wikipedia contributors. Each links to the original archive and article revision. Adapted text is available under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Word clues and places

Word clues adapt definitions from Princeton WordNet 3.0, copyright 2006 by Princeton University. Read the WordNet license and disclaimer.

Atlas questions combine city and country data from GeoNames, used under CC BY 4.0. Some culture questions use structured public-domain facts from Wikidata under CC0. Travel, cinema and landscape cards also link to the UNESCO World Heritage Centre, the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences, and the U.S. National Park Service.
