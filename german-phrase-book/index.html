Pronunciation guide

Capitals mark stressed syllables. Read ow as in “cow.” For ue, say “ee” with rounded lips. For oe, say “eh” with rounded lips. The kh sound is soft in ich and farther back in Bach. Tap Listen to hear each phrase.
