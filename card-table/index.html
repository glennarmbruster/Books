New to playing cards?

A standard deck has 52 cards in four suits: hearts ♥ and diamonds ♦ are red, clubs ♣ and spades ♠ are black. Each suit has an ace, 2 through 10, jack, queen and king. Leave out jokers unless the game asks for them.

Shuffle means mix the cards. To cut, lift a packet and put the bottom packet on top. To deal, give cards one at a time face down unless the rules say otherwise. Your hand is what you hold. The stock is the pile you draw from. The top discard is the most recent face-up card somebody threw away.

In a trick, everyone plays one card. Following suit means playing the suit that was led when you have it. Trump is a suit that beats the other suits. A meld is a combination you put together. Each chapter explains which combinations or tricks count in that game.
