How to play

Fill each row, column, and outlined 3 × 3 box with the numbers 1–9, using each number once. The dark printed numbers are fixed.

Tap a square and a number. Turn on Pencil notes to mark possible numbers. On a keyboard, use the arrows to move and Backspace to erase.
