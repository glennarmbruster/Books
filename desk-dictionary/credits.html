About the dictionary

The Desk Dictionary collects brief English definitions, including regional spellings, slang and adult vocabulary. Usage labels describe individual meanings. The index includes phrases, names and inflected forms as well as base words.

Word coverage

This independent edition draws on English Wiktionary and the historical Webster’s dictionary described below. Coverage against a complete current Merriam-Webster edition has not been verified.

English Wiktionary

English Wiktionary contributors wrote the source entries. The October 1, 2026 English dump was extracted by Kaikki.org on October 30 using Wiktextract. This edition selects and shortens meanings, retains usage labels, links spelling variants and omits examples. Locally written quick definitions and regional notes supplement selected entries.

Spelling suggestions

The search checks nearby spellings, including swapped letters. It ranks likely matches using a frequency list adapted from david47k’s English word lists, based on Google Books Ngrams. The ranking data is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0. Source, license and changes · License terms

Thesaurus

Synonyms and antonyms are grouped by meaning using English Wiktionary and Open English WordNet 2025. Related words show WordNet’s similar-adjective relationships. Tap a word to explore its meanings and check its definition for usage. The thesaurus covers a subset of dictionary entries.
