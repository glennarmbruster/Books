Adaptation: Headwords were normalized to lowercase. Recognizable parts of speech and up to three short historical senses were selected. The first sentence or an independent clause was retained. Examples, citations, indirect cross-references, transcription uncertainty, and selected outdated sensitive material were filtered. Every resulting sense carries a 1913 source label. Historical spellings and vocabulary reflect that edition. The supplementary transcription does not establish completeness against a modern Merriam-Webster edition.
