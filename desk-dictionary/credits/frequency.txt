English word frequency ranking

“Lists of the most frequently used (top) English words” by david47k, based on Google Books Ngrams English dataset (20120701).

The spelling-suggestion ranking is an adaptation of the lowercase list. Duplicate rows and entries other than lowercase words, optionally containing internal hyphens or apostrophes, were excluded. The first 60,000 remaining entries retain the source's frequency order.
